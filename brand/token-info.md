## 简介（English）

Agent Ascent (ASC) is an Agent-ID-gated fair-launch token on BNB Smart Chain. Only wallets holding a .ai Agent ID can mint. No premine, no owner, no team tokens.

Price rises 0.1 USDT every 10,000 shares (1.2 → 8 USDT). At 10,000 shares the pool opens: 90% of raised BNB plus reserve tokens go to PancakeSwap V2 and LP is burned. After launch the 10% team fee funds a 30% auto buyback-and-burn. Sell tax decays from 80% to 0 within 2 hours; 70% goes to holders as dividends, 30% is burned.

Contract source is verified on BscScan.
